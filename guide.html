Player guide · v2.4

How to play

Pick one of six heroes, explore four open-world Acts beyond the World Gate and descend the endless depths below them. Every fifth floor ends with a boss. The level cap is 50; after that, experience feeds Ascension.

Classes & resources

Paladin · Melee · StrengthValor: regenerates 4 per second. A holy knight who charges in, bounces shields between foes and calls down heaven.

Shaman · Caster · IntelligenceMana: regenerates 6.5 per second. Commands lightning, earth and spirit wolves, with totems that fight beside you.

Skills

Each class has 16 skills in four categories (Primary, Secondary, Defensive, Power) and 8 passives. Any skill fits any of the 6 slots. Skills unlock by level; passive slots open at levels 5, 15, 25, 35. Primary skills are free and most generate resource on hit; Secondary skills spend it; Defensive and Power skills usually have cooldowns. Cooldown reduction and resource cost reduction on gear cap at 50%.

Ascension

Level 50 is the cap, but experience keeps counting: every Ascension level grants one point to spend in the Ascension panel (P). Core points raise your main stat and Vitality without limit; everything else caps at 50 points. Points can be moved freely at any time.

Elements & resistances

Every enemy deals one element: Physical, Fire, Cold, Lightning, Poison, Arcane. Imps burn, cultists cast arcane, the dead hit physically; Burning, Chilling, Venomous and Charged elites deal fire, cold, poison and lightning, and each boss has its own. Resistance rolls as a secondary property on every armor piece, ring and amulet; its damage reduction is resistance ÷ (resistance + 30 + 5 × floor), capped at 75%, and stacks with armor.

Rings and amulets get one extra property. Item level follows the floor it dropped on (+1 from elites); higher item level means bigger rolls. Slots: Weapon, Helm, Chest, Gloves, Boots, two Rings, Amulet.

Weapons & off-hands

Sorcerers, Priests and Shamans carry a focus in the off-hand: it adds flat damage to every attack and rolls offensive properties. Paladins carry a shield: armor plus a chance to block (halving the hit), with defensive properties and three legendary shields. Barbarians can dual-wield two one-handed weapons (damage is averaged, attack speed +15%, both weapons’ properties count) or swing a two-handed weapon with double the damage and double-strength properties at 85% attack speed. Ashforged Barbarian weapons are always two-handed, so they can’t be dual-wielded. Rangers use two-handed bows and carry a quiver in the off-hand: flat damage, 8–15% attack speed and offensive properties, with three legendary quivers. Every class also has a legendary weapon for each of its Secondary skills.

Movement speed rolls only on boots; crit chance on rings, amulets, gloves and helms; crit damage on rings, amulets and gloves. Gems

Gem quality depends on how many Acts you have completed: none gives Rough or Cut, each Act completed raises it one step, and after all four every gem is Brilliant. Bosses drop one rank higher. Radiant gems drop only from Abyssal Trial Guardians, or are fused at the Jeweler.

Gems of the same kind and rank stack. Ranks: Rough → Cut → Polished → Fine → Brilliant → Radiant. Fuse three of a kind at the Jeweler for the next rank. Shatter a gem for 1 / 3 / 8 / 20 / 50 / 120 shards by rank; cut a new gem of any type for 2 / 6 / 16 / 40 / 100 / 240 shards. Socket a gem by clicking it in your inventory, then an item with an empty socket; the Jeweler removes gems and adds sockets.

Part 2 · Campaign

The four Acts

The World Gate in Ashen Hold leads into four open-world lands. Captain Rhea gives each Act a landmark to find, a cultist camp to clear and two lieutenants to hunt in their lairs. Once both are dead she sends you after the Act boss: click his lair to enter a dungeon with the boss at the far end, and take the portal back to the Act when he falls. Killing him opens the next Act. Finish all four and Cinder difficulty, the Abyssal Trials and the Altar of the Fallen unlock. J opens the quest log.

Part 3 · Endgame

After the campaign

Beat the Pale King and the endgame opens: Cinder difficulty, the Abyssal Trials and the Altar of the Fallen.

Cinder difficulty

The campaign is played on Normal. Once the Pale King falls, Options (Esc) lets you pick Cinder 1 to 10, but only while you stand in Ashen Hold. Each level makes monsters 55% tougher and 30% deadlier, and gives 25% more experience, 15% more gold and more legendaries. The Abyssal Trials and uber bosses need Cinder 1 or higher.

Abyssal Trials

The Abyssal Trials stone stands in the west alcove of Ashen Hold, just above the Stash. Pick a tier, slay monsters to fill the bar, then kill the Guardian that appears, all within 10 minutes. When it dies, the portal home opens at your feet. Walking over it does nothing: click it to leave, so you can spend your gem upgrades first. Each tier makes monsters about 7% tougher and 4.5% deadlier; Cinder does not change Trial difficulty. The faster you clear, the more tiers open: a clear with almost all the time left opens 9 tiers ahead, half the time left opens about 5, and a last-second clear opens 1. Clearing in time also gives Ascension experience and three tries to empower your legendary gems. Guardians often drop new legendary gems. If time runs out you can still finish for the loot.

Legendary gems

Legendary gems fit rings and amulets only, and you can wear one of each kind. Their rank has no limit. After a timed clear, each upgrade try succeeds based on the tier you cleared compared with the gem's rank: 100% when the tier is 10 or more above it, 60% at the same level, and much lower once the gem outranks the tier.

Uber bosses

On Cinder 1 or higher, each Act has a Sealbearer roaming its open world: Varn in the Ashen Reach, Mireth in the Drowned Fens, Kolgr in the Cinder Wastes and Ostra in the Pale Citadel. They are tough elites with guards, marked purple on your map, and they come back each time you enter the Act. Each drops one key (Reach, Fen, Cinder or Citadel Key). Take it to the Altar of the Fallen, on the west side of town: one key opens that Act’s uber boss in its own dungeon. Every uber drops an Ashforged weapon for your class and pieces of the uber sets.

Ashforged gear

Ashforged items have red names and perfect rolls, then every property is pushed 60% higher and their base damage or armor is 50% higher. They are always item level 100, only level 50 heroes can equip them, and Barbarian Ashforged weapons are always two-handed. Salvaging an Ashforged item gives an Ashen Core, and the Blacksmith forges an Ashforged version of any piece from your class's four sets for 3 Ashen Cores, 3 Bound Sigils, 3 Ember Cores and 25,000 gold. Uber bosses always drop an Ashforged weapon built for one of your four sets. Once you clear Trial tier 50, any legendary or set item that drops has a small chance to be Ashforged.
