Floor 1 · Install

Up and running in a minute

The installer sets everything up and adds shortcuts. There is nothing to configure.

  1. Download

    Grab AshenDepths-Setup.exe from the button above.

  2. Run the installer

    Double-click it. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info then Run anyway.

  3. Launch

    Open Ashen Depths from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut.

  4. Make a hero

    Name your hero, pick a class and head down. Press H any time for the Codex, or read the player guide.

Floor 3 · Campaign

Four Acts beyond the World Gate

The World Gate in Ashen Hold opens onto four open-world lands. Captain Rhea sends you out in each one to find a landmark, clear a cultist camp and break three boss lairs. Kill both lieutenants and the Act boss shows himself. Kill him and the next Act opens.

The Challenge Dungeons

The old stone in town stays sealed until all four Acts are done. Once the Pale King falls, it lets you jump straight to any floor you have reached and farm it again on a fresh layout.

Floor 4 · Classes

Six heroes

Each class runs on its own resource and plays differently. You can make as many heroes as you like and switch between them on the title screen.

Floor 6 · FAQ

Questions

Windows says it protected my PC. Is the installer safe?

That warning appears for new games that are not yet code-signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Only download the installer from this page or the official releases list.

How do I update?

Download the latest installer from this page and run it. It installs over the old version, and your heroes stay put.

Where are my heroes saved?

In the data folder inside the game's install folder. They are kept when you update, and uninstalling leaves that folder in place.

How do I unlock the Challenge Dungeons?

Finish all four Acts. Kill the Pale King at the end of Act IV and the stone in Ashen Hold opens the endless depths, where bosses can drop Radiant gems. It tells you how many Acts you have left until then.

Will my old hero still work after the Acts update?

Yes. Your progress moves onto Act I, so any bosses you already beat stay beaten.

Is there a browser or Mac version?

No. Ashen Depths is a Windows desktop game.

What are the controls?

WASD to move, left and right click for your main skills, 1–4 for the rest, Q potion, T town portal, I inventory, C hero, K skills, P Ascension, J quest log, M map, F11 fullscreen and Esc to pause. Every key can be rebound in Options. The player guide lists everything.
